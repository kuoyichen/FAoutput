FA_BIG: start FA with many more LIBs

A patched FA.EXE 1.02f for big mod folders. The original game crashes at startup with “Not enough memory.” once it sees more than 20 LIBs or about 10,000 files. FA_BIG raises those limits and keeps the 1.02f CRC, so launchers still work.
