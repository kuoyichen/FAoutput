Making FA LIBs that do not crash

Home-made LIBs that unpack fine everywhere can still crash FA when the mission builder opens or a mission starts. We found why by disassembling FA.EXE: it reads compressed files in 2 KB chunks straight past the end of the LIB. Five rules every LIB maker must follow, the falib tool that builds LIBs from the source files and follows them for you, and a LIB checker that runs in your browser.
