Flight setup

Right stick: pitch and roll. Left stick: up/down changes the throttle (it stays where you leave it), left/right is rudder. The sticks only answer touches inside their circles: drag anywhere else to look around (the view stays where you leave it; Front brings it back, RC view watches from the runway end like an RC model pilot), pinch to zoom, double-tap to snap the camera back. Full throttle lights the afterburner; on the carrier it also fires the catapult. The weapon button on the right picks gun, AIM-9, AIM-120 (air targets), AGM-65 (ground and ships) or MK-82 bombs; missiles lock the target nearest your nose. To land, slow down, lower the gear and raise the nose: the jet settles gently nose-high, and the throttle sets how fast it sinks.

Canyon strike: fly the winding canyon at low level. Above the rim the SAM radar sees you and the sites launch. At the end of the canyon pull up over the ridge, dive into the crater and bomb the reactor (MK-82 is selected; the yellow circle shows where the bomb falls), then climb out: the SAMs on the crater rim fire at you, so turn hard and drop flares (FLARE button or C). Get 3 nm clear to finish.

Keyboard: W/S pitch, A/D roll, Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl throttle, G gear, B brake, Space fire, F weapon, R restart, mouse drag to look, wheel to zoom.
