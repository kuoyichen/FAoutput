Flight setup

Left stick: pitch and roll. Right stick: up/down changes the throttle (it stays where you leave it), left/right is rudder. Drag anywhere else to look around, pinch to zoom, double-tap to snap the camera back. Full throttle lights the afterburner; on the carrier it also fires the catapult.

Keyboard: W/S pitch, A/D roll, Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl throttle, G gear, B brake, R restart, mouse drag to look, wheel to zoom.
