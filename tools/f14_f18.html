New F-14 and F/A-18, now with their weapons

全新外型的 F-14 與 F/A-18，會畫出自己掛的武器

Two replacement shapes for Fighters Anthology: F14.SH with a rebuilt nose, and F18.SH reshaped like the FAshion Hornets. Both now draw the missiles, bombs and fuel tanks the aircraft really carries in the game. Stores disappear as you fire them, pylons stay, and dropped tanks fall away. Try any loadout below, then download the files.

兩個給 Fighters Anthology 用的替換外形：機鼻重做的 F14.SH，以及照 FAshion Hornet 修整過的 F18.SH。兩架現在都會畫出飛機在遊戲裡實際掛的飛彈、炸彈和副油箱。武器射出就消失，派龍架留著，拋掉的油箱會往下掉。下面可以自己換掛載看看，再下載檔案。

What changed from the factory SH

Both files are edits of the original FA shapes, not new models. The low-detail models and the moving parts (gear, flaps, hook, swing wings) are the factory ones. The textures are the factory ones plus three afterburner flame frames in unused space.

兩個檔案都是改自 FA 原廠外形，不是全新的模型。遠距離低細節模型和會動的零件（起落架、襟翼、尾鉤、可變翼）都是原廠的。貼圖是原廠的，只在空白處加了三格後燃器火焰。

F-14 Tomcat F14.SH

  • New nose. The radome was rebuilt from smooth rings so the tip sits on the fuselage centreline, as on the real jet, instead of drooping. The black radome paint and the pitot are gone, and the top and bottom lines blend smoothly into the fuselage. It is 57 points and 94 faces with computed shading normals, so it looks round in the game.
  • Chin pod moved back about 5 ft, with its own drawing-order split so the pod and nose never show through each other.
  • Weapons drawn from the real loadout: four AIM-7s in the belly tunnel, two fuel tanks under the intakes, two AIM-54s on the glove pylons and two AIM-9s on the pylon shoulders. Any station can also carry the other store types (AIM-120, HARM, GBU, Mk-82, JDAM).
  • Its own AIM-54 (octagonal body, straight cone nose, Phoenix fins). The other weapons are FA's own weapon models at their real size. AIM-7s have a blue band, and the tanks sit slightly nose-up to fit under the nacelles.
  • Glove pylons with a raked leading edge. Pylons stay after firing and disappear when the station is empty.
  • Jettisoned or empty tanks fall away for 2 seconds, nose dropping about the tail.
  • 3D landing gear: struts with slim braces, a nose-gear housing and piston, and round wheels with an octagonal tyre. The wheels spin in flight-sim style: still at 0 kt, faster with speed, and stopped again above 180 kt.
  • Parked wing oversweep: stopped on the ground or a carrier deck with the gear down, the wings sweep back to 75° over 4 seconds, as the real F-14 parks. They spread again in 3 seconds when you move off, or at full afterburner on the catapult even though the jet is not moving yet.
  • Flaps: with the flaps down and the wings fully spread, the trailing-edge strip of each wing drops about 33° and leaves a notch in the wing, as on the real jet. With the wings swept, the flaps still work but are not drawn.
  • GBU-16 shape is the same balanced body with four X fins as on the F/A-18.
  • Bombs and air-to-ground missiles (Nov 21): Mk-82, Mk-83, Mk-84 and CBU loads are drawn with the redrawn, symmetric Mk-84, and every AGM (HARM included) with the AGM-65 Maverick shape.
  • Same upgrades on three more F-14s: F14J.SH (Jolly Rogers: black tails with the skull, black nose band and spine, black tanks, white flaps), FIR.SH (Iranian tan) and FBC.SH (Black Cat), each with its own texture. Pick them above the viewer.

  • 下巴的莢艙往後移大約 5 呎，並加上自己的繪製順序切分，莢艙和機鼻不會互相透出來。
  • 依實際掛載畫出武器：機腹凹槽四枚 AIM-7、進氣道下方兩個副油箱、翼套派龍上兩枚 AIM-54、派龍側軌上兩枚 AIM-9。每個掛點也都能掛其他種類（AIM-120、HARM、GBU、Mk-82、JDAM）。
  • 自己做的 AIM-54（八角形彈身、直錐形彈頭、鳳凰飛彈的尾翼）。其他武器用 FA 原本的武器模型，維持實際大小。AIM-7 有藍色色帶，副油箱略為抬頭，好貼合發動機艙下方。
  • 翼套派龍前緣做成斜的。武器射完派龍架還在，掛點清空才會消失。

  • 立體起落架：輪柱加細斜撐、前輪筒身和活塞，輪子是圓形貼圖加八角形輪胎。輪子會轉：0 節不轉、愈快轉愈快、超過 180 節又停下。
  • 停機時主翼後掠：在地面或航艦甲板上停住、起落架放下時，主翼在 4 秒內後掠到 75 度，跟真的 F-14 停機一樣。開始移動時 3 秒內展開；在彈射器上推到全後燃器時，即使還沒動也會展開。
  • 襟翼：襟翼放下而且主翼全展時，每邊主翼後緣那一條會往下轉約 33 度，主翼留下缺口，跟真機一樣。主翼後掠時襟翼照常作用，但不畫出來。

F/A-18 Hornet F18.SH

  • Reshaped like the FAshion F/A-18C: narrower and lower fuselage, lower canopy, longer nose and tail, smaller vertical and horizontal tails. The two-seat cockpit is kept. See the F/A-18 Showcase for the whole family.
  • Gaps closed at the wing root and the LEX, and the main gear doors are half their old height.
  • Weapons drawn from the real loadout: a centreline tank, AIM-120s on the fuselage, GBUs inboard, HARMs outboard and AIM-9s on the wingtips by default. Every station can take any store type (tank, AIM-9, AIM-7, AIM-120, AIM-54, HARM, GBU, Mk-82, JDAM).
  • FA's own weapon models at their own size, hung on the hardpoints of the new F18.PT. The 480-gallon tank has a pointed nose and tail cone.
  • Wing stores moved 4 units back so their tails clear the lowered flaps. Wing pylons hide when a station is empty.
  • Jettisoned or empty tanks fall away from the centreline and both wing stations. Only fuel tanks fall; fired missiles just disappear.
  • 3D landing gear: the nose strut now reaches the fuselage, with a housing, piston, collar, fork block and launch bar, and the wheels spin with speed (still at 0 kt, stopped above 180 kt).
  • Parked wing fold: stopped on the ground or a carrier deck with the gear down, the outer wings fold up over 4 seconds. They spread again in 3 seconds when you move off, or at full afterburner on the catapult.
  • Fuselage AIM-120s moved forward so their noses sit just behind the intake lip, as on the real jet.
  • Bombs and air-to-ground missiles (Nov 21): Mk-82, Mk-84 and CBU loads are drawn with the redrawn, symmetric Mk-84, AGM-65 Maverick has its own shape, HARM keeps its own, and an AIM-54 (which the Hornet never carries) is drawn as an AIM-120.

Under the hood

技術細節

FA itself never draws stores on an aircraft. These shapes do it on their own: small x86 routines inside the SH read the aircraft's stations (weapon type and rounds left) every frame and pick which store to draw. New drawing-order (BSP) plane nodes are chained into the original tree, so stores show correctly behind or in front of the wings and fuselage from every angle. The CODE section had to grow to hold all of it.

FA 本身從來不會在飛機上畫外掛，這兩個外形是自己畫的：SH 裡的小段 x86 程式每一格都去讀飛機的掛點（武器種類和剩餘數量），決定要畫哪一種外掛。原本的繪製順序樹（BSP）裡串進了新的分割平面節點，所以從任何角度看，外掛在機翼和機身前面或後面都會正確。為了放下這些東西，CODE 區段加大了。

How to install

  1. Download F14.SH + F14.PT + _F14.PIC and/or F18.SH + F18.PT + _F18.PIC (or the zip with all six).
  2. Back up any files with the same names, then copy the new ones to where your FA install loads add-on SH, PT and PIC files.
  3. Start a mission in the F-14 or F/A-18, pick a loadout in the arming screen, and look at your jet in an outside view.
  1. 下載 F14.SH + F14.PT + _F14.PIC，以及／或 F18.SH + F18.PT + _F18.PIC（或一次下載六個檔案的 zip）。
  2. 先備份同名的舊檔，再把新檔放到你的 FA 讀取外加 SH、PT、PIC 檔的地方。
  3. 開一場 F-14 或 F/A-18 的任務，在掛載畫面選武器，再切到外部視角看你的飛機。
Use the matching PT. The SH draws a store by station number, so it needs the station layout of the new PT. With the stock F14.PT the F-14 shows two AIM-54s on one side and none on the other, and no AIM-9s. Each variant has its own PT: F14D.PT also flies F14.SH, F14J.PT flies F14J.SH, F14I.PT flies FIR.SH and F14X.PT flies FBC.SH; the F/A-18 variants are on the Showcase page. The PT hardpoints are also where FA launches the weapons, and some are set a little away from where the SH draws them on purpose, so missiles leave clear of the airframe.
要用對應的 PT。SH 是依掛點編號畫外掛，所以需要新 PT 的掛點配置。如果用原廠 F14.PT，F-14 會一邊畫兩枚 AIM-54、另一邊沒有，也沒有 AIM-9。每個衍生型都有自己的 PT：F14D.PT 也用 F14.SH，F14J.PT 用 F14J.SH，F14I.PT 用 FIR.SH，F14X.PT 用 FBC.SH；F/A-18 各型請看展示頁。PT 的掛點也是 FA 發射武器的位置，有幾個是故意設得離 SH 畫的位置遠一點，讓飛彈離開機身後才射出。
