How to use it with your own AI

用你自己的 AI 執行

  1. Give your AI three files: the skill, one items file that matches your FA install, and the .MM.txt of your map. In Claude, put them in a Project or attach them to a chat. Use a strong model: the skill is long (about 60,000 tokens).給 AI 三個檔案：skill、一個和你的 FA 安裝相符的 items 檔，以及地圖的 .MM.txt。在 Claude 可以放進 Project 或附加在對話裡。請用強一點的模型：skill 很長（約 6 萬 token）。
  2. Send this first message once:先送一次這段訊息： 
    Follow the attached Fighters Anthology mission generator skill exactly. Use the attached items file as the asset catalog and the attached .MM.txt as the map reference. If the items file is marked "JKP600" instead of "JKPFA_53b", treat it as the DEFAULT_PLUS_JKPFA_53B profile (JKP 600 is the newer JKP library). Give me the .M and .MT as downloadable files.
  3. Describe the mission, for example:描述任務，例如： 
    Taiwan map. I fly an F-16 from Chiayi with three wingmen at 20,000 ft. Four MiG-29s come from the west. Destroy them; Chiayi airbase must survive.
    台灣地圖。我駕駛 F-16 帶三架僚機從嘉義起飛，高度 20,000 呎。四架 MiG-29 從西邊來，擊落它們，嘉義基地必須存活。
  4. Copy the .M and .MT into your FA folder and pick the mission in FA. Ask the AI for real files, not text to copy: a .M has 0x01 bytes around names and ends with a 0x00 byte, which a copied text block loses. To change the mission, ask in the same chat; the name keeps its six-letter stem and the last character counts up (TAIWAN1 → TAIWAN2).把 .M 和 .MT 放進 FA 目錄，在 FA 裡選這個任務。請 AI 給真正的檔案，不要複製文字：.M 的名稱前後有 0x01 位元組、檔尾是 0x00，複製文字會遺失。要修改就在同一個對話裡說，檔名前六個字不變，最後一個字遞增（TAIWAN1 → TAIWAN2）。
