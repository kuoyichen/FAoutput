This page works like the Mission Creator inside FA: pick an object on the map and change it by clicking the boxed words on the right. Drag objects and waypoints to move them, drag empty map to scroll, use the wheel or the small slider to zoom. OK saves the .M mission and its .MT briefing; put both in your FA folder. Everything stays in your browser. Only settings that are decoded in the mission skill can be changed; anything else in an opened file is kept as it was.

這個頁面的用法和 FA 內建的 Mission Creator 一樣：在地圖上點選物件，再點右邊方框裡的字來修改。拖曳物件和航點可以移動，拖曳空白地圖可以捲動，用滾輪或小滑桿縮放。按 OK 會存出 .M 任務檔和 .MT 簡報檔，兩個都放進 FA 目錄即可。所有東西都只在你的瀏覽器裡處理。只有任務 skill 裡已解碼的設定可以修改；開啟的檔案裡其他內容都會原樣保留。
